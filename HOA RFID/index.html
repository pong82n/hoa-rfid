SFSPh1 Regulations

HOA Vehicle & RFID Access Guidelines

Frequently asked questions regarding RFID decal issuance, gate speed limits, and visitor policies.

How to Claim Your RFID Decal

Once your vehicle is registered online, present your vehicle at the SFSPh1 HOA Admin Office (Mon-Sat, 8AM - 5PM) for authorized installation by security personnel on the designated windshield position.

Subdivision Speed Limit

Strict 20 km/h speed limit is enforced across all Phase 1 avenues and streets. Please slow down when approaching barrier gates to allow the long-range RFID reader to register your tag safely.

Replacement & Tamper Policy

RFID tags have tamper-evident die-cuts. Any attempt to peel or transfer the sticker between vehicles will permanently disable the antenna. Replacement stickers cost ₱250 upon submission of an affidavit.
